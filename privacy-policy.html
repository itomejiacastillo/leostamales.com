Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

At Leo's Tamales, we respect your privacy. This website serves customers in Los Angeles County, California, and explains what information we collect when you place an order or pre-order.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information you voluntarily provide when you submit our online order form or contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your email address.
  • Your order details, delivery city, and requested date.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to reply to your inquiry and to confirm, prepare, and deliver your order. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Netlify Forms: processes and delivers your online order form submissions to us.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: protects the form from spam and automated bots.
  • Google Fonts: serves the website typography.

4. Cookies

This website does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. We only remember your chosen language in your browser so you don't have to pick it every time.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any information you sent us by emailing leostamales@gmail.com.
